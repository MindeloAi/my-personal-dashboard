# Restaurant Proposal Playbook (Trinidad & Tobago)

This is the source of truth for drafting website + AI-automation proposals for **restaurants, bars, and food spots in Trinidad & Tobago**. The `/draft-proposal` command reads a lead, then writes a tailored proposal into the lead's **Proposal Draft** field using this playbook.

> **Human-led sales.** Claude only *drafts*. A human reviews and sends every proposal. Never message a prospect directly.

---

## Who this is for

T&T food businesses: dine-in restaurants, roti shops, bars/limes, doubles vendors going digital, cafés, caterers, and ghost kitchens. They usually already sell on **WhatsApp** and Instagram and want a real website + smoother ordering, not an enterprise platform.

## Local context (use this — it builds trust)

- **Currency:** quote in **TTD** ($). If the lead is diaspora/USD, note both.
- **Ordering reality:** most orders come through **WhatsApp** and phone. A site should *feed* WhatsApp, not fight it — "Order on WhatsApp" buttons that pre-fill the order/message.
- **Payments:** card gateways in T&T are limited and slow to approve. Default to **WhatsApp order → pay on pickup/delivery or bank transfer/Linx**. Offer online card payment as a phase-2 add-on, not a blocker.
- **Delivery:** many use their own driver or services like local couriers — keep delivery zones/fees simple and editable.
- **Hours & holidays:** Carnival, Divali, Eid, Christmas affect hours; make hours easy for the owner to update themselves.
- **Mobile-first:** the vast majority of T&T traffic is mobile data. Pages must be fast and light.

## What we sell (positioning)

A clean, fast, mobile-first website that **turns Instagram/WhatsApp interest into orders**, plus optional AI automation that answers FAQs and captures orders 24/7 — so the owner stops losing late-night messages.

---

## Package tiers

Pick the tier that matches the lead's **Budget Range** and **Service Interest**. Adjust line items to what they actually asked for in their **Message**.

### 1. Menu Site — *starter* (≈ TTD $2,500–$4,000 one-off)
- Mobile-first one-page or small multi-page site
- Digital menu (categories, prices, photos) the owner can update
- "Order on WhatsApp" buttons with pre-filled messages
- Hours, location/map, contact, Instagram feed link
- Basic SEO + Google Business setup
- **Timeline:** ~1–2 weeks

### 2. Ordering Site — *most popular* (≈ TTD $4,000–$7,000 one-off + optional hosting/care plan)
- Everything in Starter, plus:
- Structured online ordering cart → sends order to WhatsApp / email
- Delivery zones & fees, pickup scheduling
- Specials / promo banner the owner controls
- Optional online card payment (phase 2)
- **Timeline:** ~2–4 weeks

### 3. Site + AI Automation — *growth* (Ordering Site + TTD $1,000–$2,500/mo automation)
- Everything in Ordering, plus:
- AI WhatsApp/Instagram assistant: answers menu/hours/delivery questions 24/7, captures orders, hands off to a human when needed
- Auto follow-ups for abandoned orders and reviews
- Monthly report on orders/enquiries
- **Timeline:** site in 2–4 weeks; automation layered in after launch

### Care plan (optional, any tier)
Hosting, edits, menu/price updates, monitoring: **TTD $250–$600/mo**.

## Proposal structure (what `/draft-proposal` writes into Proposal Draft)

Keep it warm, concrete, and short — owners are busy. Use TTD. Reference *their* business by name.

1. **Greeting** — by name, reference their business and what they said in their Message.
2. **What we understand you need** — 2–3 sentences mirroring their goal.
3. **What we'd build** — the recommended tier as bullet points, tied to their reality (WhatsApp ordering, mobile, T&T payments).
4. **Investment** — the tier price in TTD (one-off + any monthly), stated plainly. Offer the care plan.
5. **Timeline** — realistic range.
6. **Next step** — "Reply and we'll set up a quick call / WhatsApp chat to confirm details." (The human sends this.)

## Tone

Trinidadian-friendly and professional. Confident, not corporate. No jargon. Short paragraphs. It should read like a real person who knows the local food scene wrote it.

## Don'ts

- Don't promise card-payment go-live dates — gateway approval is out of our hands.
- Don't over-scope. Recommend the tier that fits their budget; mention upgrades as "later."
- Don't auto-send. Draft only.
